01 Charter

Eight things
we are trying
to build in you.

Our charter, in plain language. Every session, outing and workshop traces back to one of these eight lines.

PICT · DHANKAWADI · PUNE 411043

  1. OBJ.01

    Develop life skills

    Build the soft skills a degree never examines, through timeless wisdom.

  2. OBJ.02

    Unlock individual potential

    Sharpen concentration, motivation and self-confidence until they hold under pressure.

  3. OBJ.03

    Build character & competence

    Competence opens the door. Character decides how long you stay in the room.

  4. OBJ.04

    Promote life balance

    Hold personal, professional and entertainment priorities in proportion.

  5. OBJ.05

    Encourage healthy living

    Stay clear of the habits and addictions that quietly cost people their twenties.

  6. OBJ.06

    Cultivate self-awareness

    Develop sensitivity and self-awareness in ordinary, everyday situations.

  7. OBJ.07

    Broaden perspectives

    Sit with international faculty and find out how much larger the world is.

  8. OBJ.08

    Experience nature

    Get out of Pune, up a hill, and remember what quiet sounds like.

02 Programs

VIBE is where every member starts. IMPACT is what you earn by showing up.

VIBE
